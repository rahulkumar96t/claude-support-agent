# Payments and refunds timing

## Accepted payment methods

Acme Trail Supply accepts major credit and debit cards and store gift cards.

## When does a refund arrive

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear on the
statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the card issuer. Support cannot send a
refund to a different card or account.

## Price adjustments

If the price of an item drops within 14 days of delivery, customers can ask for the
difference through a support ticket. Clearance items are excluded from price adjustments.
