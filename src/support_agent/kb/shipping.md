# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days after an order leaves the warehouse. Express
shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm warehouse time usually ship
the same business day.

## Carriers and tracking

Orders ship with Parcelwise, a fictional carrier used in this demo. A tracking number is
available as soon as the order status changes to shipped. Tracking events can take up to
24 hours to appear after the label is printed.

## Late or lost packages

If a package has not moved for 5 business days, or is more than 3 business days past the
estimated delivery date, support opens a carrier investigation ticket. Investigations take
up to 7 business days. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, the order is reshipped
or refunded in full.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed only while the order is still processing. Once the
order has shipped, the address can no longer be changed by support.
