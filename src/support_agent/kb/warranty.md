# Warranty

## Coverage

Tents, backpacks, sleeping bags and headlamps carry a 1-year warranty against
manufacturing defects, starting on the delivery date. Normal wear, misuse and accidental
damage are not covered.

## How to make a warranty claim

Warranty claims are handled through a support ticket. The ticket should include the order
number, a short description of the defect, and when it was first noticed. A specialist
replies within 2 business days and may ask for photos.

## Warranty versus refund

A refund is for items returned within the refund window. A warranty claim is for defects
that appear later. If an item is still inside its refund window, a refund is usually faster.
